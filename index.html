How long does a website take?

Business websites take 7 days. Systems and software take 3–4 weeks. We agree a launch date before we start.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once paid for, the design, code, domain and hosting are all yours.

How does payment work?

Half to start, half at launch. Mobile Money, bank transfer or international payment, agreed in writing first.

What does “revisions until you love it” cover?

Design changes before launch, within the pages and features agreed at the start. New pages or features are quoted separately.

Can you run ads for us too?

Yes. We plan and manage Meta and Google Ads, on their own or as part of the Scale package.

Do you work with clients around the world?

Yes. We’ve delivered projects in Ghana, Canada and the UAE, and we take on clients anywhere. We work over WhatsApp, email and video calls, and can quote in US dollars.
