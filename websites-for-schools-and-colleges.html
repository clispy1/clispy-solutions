Can the website take admission enquiries?

Yes. A short enquiry form built for phones, plus WhatsApp and call buttons, so families can reach you the way they prefer.

Can we change intake dates and fees ourselves?

Yes. We have built for education and training clients in Ghana and the UAE, and we work with organisations anywhere. We work over WhatsApp, email and video calls, and can quote in US dollars.

A business website takes 7 days from the start, on a launch date agreed in writing. Systems and software, such as a student portal, take 3–4 weeks.
